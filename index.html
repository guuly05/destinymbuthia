04 // FAQS

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I request an appointment for a shoot?

Simply visit the Inquire Page or click any package button to send Destiny a message with your preferred dates and shoot details. Destiny responds personally to confirm availability.

What is the turnaround time for 35mm film scans?

35mm film requires specialized lab processing and high-res scanning. Digital previews/edits are usually delivered within 3-5 days, while film scans are delivered within 7-10 days.

Do you offer styling & creative direction?

Yes! Destiny has 3 years of experience in editing, styling, and directing. All Standard and Deluxe sessions include wardrobe styling consultation and location guidance.
