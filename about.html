02 // ABOUT

Behind the Lens

"What makes my photography special is the intention behind details. In my craft, you can tell I have an eye for lighting and a story. My work is creative and authentic."

My name is Destiny Mbuthia and I'm a 21-year-old freelance photographer and student based in Fayetteville, Arkansas. Originally from Uganda, I study journalism at the University of Arkansas.

I have three years of experience in editing, directing, and styling. My work blends the rich, organic soul of 35mm film with the precision and clarity of digital photography.

I am proud of my portfolio, and I hope you can appreciate it too.
